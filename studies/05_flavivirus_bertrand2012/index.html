1. Literature Provenance & Cryptographic Authenticity

Mammalian tick-borne flaviviruses (MTBFG)—including Tick-Borne Encephalitis Virus (TBEV), Louping Ill Virus (LIV), and Omsk Hemorrhagic Fever Virus (OHFV)—were long considered an exclusively clonal viral group incapable of viable homologous recombination. In 2012, Bertrand et al. presented the first rigorous evolutionary authentication and dating of a natural recombination event in MTBFG. Using RDP3 and BEAST coalescent modeling, the authors demonstrated that Louping ill virus possesses a recombinant cassette in the NS3 helicase domain (subdomain 3) derived from a Western TBEV (Neudoerfl-like) ancestor inserted into a Spanish sheep encephalomyelitis virus (SSEV) backbone, dating the crossover event to 76–282 years before present.

4. Biological Narrative & Evolutionary Mechanism

The Louping ill virus horizontal exchange is a classic double-crossover cassette event. Rather than swapping entire functional proteins (as in the HCV RF1_2k/1b NS2/NS3 boundary or the SARS-CoV-2 Spike RBD), this event inserted a circumscribed domain within the NS3 RNA helicase (subdomain 3).

Why did historical tools disagree by over 100 nucleotides on where this insert began and ended? Between alignment positions 5799 and 5906 (107 nt), only a single nucleotide substitution distinguishes the parental lineages. Heuristic algorithms that rely on fixed window sizes (e.g., 25 nt vs. 100 nt) fluctuate wildly across this uninformative stretch, reporting arbitrary cutpoints based on window weighting. In contrast, RhizAeon explicitly demarcates the entire uninformative plateau $[5799, 5906]$, confirming that the published RDP consensus (Aln 5806) resides within this flat likelihood manifold. On the 3' exit flank, dense parent-specific SNPs immediately constrain the exit junction to an exact 11-nt plateau $[6222, 6233]$, improving likelihood by $\Delta \ln L = +28.47$.
