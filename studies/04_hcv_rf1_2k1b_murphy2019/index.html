1. Literature Provenance & Cryptographic Authenticity

Hepatitis C virus Circulating Recombinant Form 1 (RF1_2k/1b), originally isolated in St. Petersburg, Russia (Kalinina et al. 2002 J. Virol.), is the only epidemiologically widespread intergenotypic recombinant in HCV history. In 2019, Hostager et al. (Wertheim laboratory, UCSD) conducted a comprehensive phylodynamic investigation using RDP4/5 and BEAST v1.10.4, establishing that all global 2k/1b isolates descend from a single ancestral recombination event emerging circa 1946 (95% HPD: 1944.7–1947.1). Using the RDP suite, the authors localized the joint recombination breakpoint to position 3,187 of reference strain H77 (95% confidence interval: 3,172–3,202), directly bridging the NS2 and NS3 genes.

4. Biological Narrative & Evolutionary Mechanism

The HCV RF1_2k/1b lineage represents a textbook example of natural virological modularity. The 5' portion of the genome encodes the structural polyprotein Core, E1, E2, the p7 viroporin, and the N-terminal catalytic core of the NS2 protease, all inherited from subtype 2k. The 3' portion encodes the non-structural enzymatic replication complex—including the NS3 serine protease and RNA helicase, NS4A cofactor, NS4B replication complex organizer, NS5A phosphoprotein, and NS5B RNA-dependent RNA polymerase—inherited from subtype 1b.

Crucially, the crossover does not disrupt the NS2/NS3 auto-proteolytic cleavage site (amino acids 1026–1027, corresponding to alignment nt 3,330), which is essential for releasing mature NS3. Instead, the crossover occurs ~215 nucleotides upstream inside the C-terminal coding sequence of NS2. RhizAeon demonstrates that between alignment position 3108 and 3122, the 2k parent, 1b parent, and recombinant isolates are identical across all sites, creating an invariant 14-nucleotide likelihood plateau. Single-base profile polishing establishes that the biological crossover could have occurred at any point within this 14-nt window without altering the primary sequence of the recombinant virus, bounded exactly by informative polymorphisms at nt 3108 (2k allele) and nt 3123 (1b allele).
